## Key Points

Privacy by Default, Not by Policy

Minute is designed so your meeting context stays on your Mac. No bots joining calls, no cloud transcription pipeline, and no hidden data brokerage layer behind the scenes.

Local Processing

Audio capture, analysis, and note generation happen on-device. Your recordings and outputs remain in your own storage and workflow.

No Mandatory Accounts

Use Minute without creating an external account or routing your notes through third-party dashboards just to get basic functionality.

Transparent Outputs

Notes are generated as readable Markdown and structured metadata you can inspect, edit, and move. No black-box document format lock-in.

Privacy claims should be verifiable. Minute keeps this practical: local-first architecture, direct files, and clear export paths.
